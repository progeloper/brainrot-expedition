Generated by `python3 -I tools/camp-rework/economy_sim.py --write` (400 Monte-Carlo runs per scenario, medians; fixed seeds, so reruns are identical).

| This model, calibrated | 247 s | 4.29 h | 2.83 h |

Calibration knob: **effective trip seconds** (the 150 s trip plus everything the doc's 150 s leaves out: walking to the gate/shop, banking, selling, re-equipping, idle). With the documented 150 s the model is faster than the doc (3.56 h vs 4.3 h), so the trip was stretched to **247 s** at base cart speed. The 2x-pass result is not calibrated separately; compare it with the documented 2.6 h as a check of the model's shape.

## 3. Recommendation

- **Keep Level1Xp = 60, growth 1.35.** Level1Xp moves the Jungle level by only about one level per +/-15 XP, because roller multipliers (Steel x3 ... Rocket x40, bought before Jungle) dominate XP income. At 60 the Jungle level is 10 at 10 s, 11 at 15 s, 12 at 20 s, 13 at 30 s, 14 at 45 s.
- **Design assumption: ~10 s of training per trip on average** (for example one ~1 min session every 6 trips). That lands level ~10 (x1.20 speed, = old Speed 2) at Jungle with Level1Xp 60. A player who trains 20 s every trip is ~2 levels ahead, which is fine because speed is capped at x1.5 (level 25).
- Training costs the free player roughly +12 min to Glitch at 20 s/trip versus the Speed baseline: the time on the rollers does not hunt, and the speed multiplier arrives later than the purchased Speed levels did. The game is income-bound (slots and gates), so cart speed is a small lever either way (no training at all is only ~18 min slower than baseline). One untested lever if parity matters: the old Speed-1 price point (500M) currently has no roller equivalent.

## 4. Assumptions and missing data

- Free player always hunts the newest unlocked region (pools are strictly better further along).
- A trip is one cart run: catches = 75% of cargo capacity in expectation (3 + Cargo level; the fractional part is a Bernoulli draw), drawn by the region's tier weights, uniform species within a tier (GlitchZone override BRT_031 2999 : BRT_032 1), 4% mutation chance with MutationCatalog shares.
- Trip time = effective trip x (60% / speed multiplier + 40%). The 60% driving share is an assumption (sensitivity table covers 40-80%). Effective trip is the calibration knob (documented 150 s gave a faster baseline than the doc).
- Every catch is banked (counts toward gate rarity requirements as a distinct species, matching WorldProgressionRules.Count). The base keeps the best N by income (N = 12 + 6 per expansion); anything displaced or worse is sold for its sale value (60 x workbook cps x 500,000 x mutation).
- Income accrues continuously (as with Auto-Collect / prompt collection): sum of slot cps x mutation x (1 + 0.10 x rebirths) x 2 with the 2x Cash pass. No VIP, Cash Boosts, Gems, crates, daily rewards, Starter Pack, rare waves or quests.
- Purchases are checked at camp after each trip, in priority order: (1) next region gate when its price and rarity branch are met; (2) next Cargo level; (3) next Speed level (baseline) or next roller (Training); (4) next base expansion. Upgrades (2-3) are bought only if the price is <= 25% of the next gate price or <= 10 min of current income, so they never stall a gate.
- Expansions use the Cash route, which zeroes the wallet (Phase13Rules.ApplyRebirth) and adds +6 slots and +10% income; bought only with a full base while the wallet is within 1.25 x the price and below the next gate. Collection-route expansions, base designs, carts and weapons are not modelled.
- Training: after every trip the player spends a fixed number of seconds on the rollers (no hunting; income still accrues). XP = seconds x roller multiplier (x2 only with the separate Training 2x pass, which the '2x pass' rows do NOT include; they own the 2x Cash pass only). Level-ups use Training.XpToNext; speed uses Training.SpeedMultiplier. Rollers are bought at Steel 2.5B, Turbo 15B, Rocket 75B, Rift 300B, in the same priority slot Speed had; the old Speed 1 price point (500M) has no roller equivalent.
- Weapons, shot time, escape timers, other players and competition for spawns are folded into the effective trip time; capture always succeeds for whatever fills the cargo.
- Not found in the repo: the original simulation script, its trip-time breakdown (drive vs hunt), its purchase priority, and whether it used expansions or designs. The documented 2.6 h for the 2x pass is reproduced only approximately (the model gives the value in section 0).

Calibrated effective trip: 247 s at base cart speed, of which 60% scales with 1/speed.
