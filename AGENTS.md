# Owner override — 10 November 2026, drive-by hunting loop

The owner approved the drive-by redesign on 10 November 2026. It **supersedes** the 24 October sentence "Do not invent proximity acquisition or automatic banking" below. Current rules, tuned in `src/shared/Config/HuntFlow.lua`:

- Acquisition is still **shooting to sleep** with the server-validated shot. A sleeping brainrot beside the owner's driven cart is loaded into a free cargo slot by the server (`BrainrotInteractionSystem.TryScoopIntoCargo`, driven by `DriveByHaulSystem`). Clients never name a scoop target.
- Cargo banks onto free platforms when the driven cart arrives at the owner's base (`TryBankCargoItem`, through the existing `BaseStorageSystem:TryDepositItem`). On-foot E pickup, cargo loading and manual platform placement all remain.
- Wild brainrots spawn ahead of the hunter with the fewest nearby when a validated spot exists, never depart beside a player or within 8 s of a hit, and stranded ones are recycled toward starved hunters.
- The 5-minute cycle no longer wipes the world, kills players or forfeits catches; it starts a rare wave (`ResetWipesWorld = false`). Only Epic and above have escape timers.
- Weapon/item IDs, prices, ownership, receipts and first-bank retention rules are unchanged.
